ButterflyDreaming

An experiment in social media and poetic identity.

◆ ◆ ◆

Anonymous, free, moderated. No sign-in, no tracking, no charge. Contributions are moderated for toxic content and to ensure privacy and anonymity.

Navigate through the graph — through nodes of interests, moods and thoughts to seeds of text pieces. Use these to exchange text with another user and possibly save into the graph. Explore text-driven generative art, music and other media and export out of the graph — all content is public domain and free. The platform is designed for embedding in external works. We suggest the graph might evolve into a sort of “poetic identity” of which we can all be part.
